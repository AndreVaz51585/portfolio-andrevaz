EvoLab as a Service

A multi-user platform around OpenEvolve that turns LLM-driven algorithm evolution into a configurable, observable product. Users can connect OpenAI, Gemini, Anthropic or local models, upload evaluators, launch isolated experiments and follow fitness, checkpoints and best solutions. Google OAuth, BCrypt password hashing and encrypted provider credentials protect the workspace.
